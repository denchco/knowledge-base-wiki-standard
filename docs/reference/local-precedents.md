## Anti-rabbit-hole handoff synthesis

The 2026-08-03 audit covered all immediate child repositories. No child had semantic runtime enforcement or a Claude-specific mirror. The reusable composite is therefore instruction-led and statically checked:

1. DMedC supplies the goal/cycle gate and one-pass completion test.
2. InterSystems excludes passive waits, trigger-only entries, generic maintenance, and background-control documents.
3. READ-SNOMED records parked items with explicit reopen conditions and keeps them out of recurring handoffs.
4. EPMA supplies the concise boundary against adjacent rabbit holes without explicit scope.

The Standard tightens these precedents by making three a maximum rather than an unconditional quota. Active project status may still hold three priorities, but an answer never invents a third recommendation or treats breadth as merely interesting adjacent exploration.
