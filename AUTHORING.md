# Authoring policy

Use **ASD-STE100-inspired writing at 80%** for new original English Wiki prose. The [writing specification](docs/spec/writing-style.md) defines this DenchCo policy under `DKBWS-PROMPT-001`.

The 80% target is a pragmatic style preference, not a computed score or a claim of ASD-STE100 compliance. Apply the main writing habits by default. Preserve technical precision and reader comprehension when an exception is needed. Do not manufacture a quota of exceptions.

- Use familiar words, consistent terms and clear sentence structures.
- Use direct commands for procedures. Put necessary conditions before actions and normally give one action per step.
- Prefer active voice when the actor is known. Never invent an actor to avoid passive voice.
- Aim for no more than 20 words per procedural sentence and 25 per descriptive sentence. Develop one topic per paragraph, normally within six sentences.
- Keep explanations connected. Use lists, tables and short sentences when they help the reader.
- Define unfamiliar abbreviations and technical terms. Retain precise domain language and the project's established spelling convention.

Review new and replaced passages before completing the change. Preserve evidence state, uncertainty, attribution, obligations and exceptions. Record any material style variation and its reason in this policy or the change record.

Preserve required exact quotations, source titles and identities, canonical governing questions, official names, code, commands, URLs, paths and identifiers. Apply the style to the surrounding explanation. This policy does not authorise source corrections or changes to mandated wording.

Explicit user instructions and stronger local rules take precedence. Existing content has no bulk rewrite obligation. A stricter STE contract needs its own appropriate review. The checks in this repository verify instruction propagation; they do not certify the prose or calculate an 80% compliance result.

The current reference is Issue 9, dated 15 January 2025. Official evidence and research limits are registered in the specification. Do not copy the copyrighted dictionary or rulebook into the repository.
