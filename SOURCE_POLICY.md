# Source, Copyright, and Data Policy

Every source record should state identity, origin, retrieval date, source type, licence or usage basis, confidentiality, personal-data classification, retention location, integrity/hash where useful, and known limitations.

When a reader-facing page directly names and links an official authority, guidance, or publication, use the primary URL registered in that source record. Displayed stable source identities link internally to their exact source-register rows; the register remains the governed hop to external authority.

## Storage tiers

- **Raw**: immutable originals. Large, sensitive, licensed, or binary material may remain outside Git in controlled object storage with a content hash and retrieval record.
- **Staging**: deterministic or reviewable extraction tied one-to-one to raw identity. Regenerable output need not be canonical.
- **Canonical wiki**: reviewed synthesis and structured records in Git.
- **Generated outputs**: rendered, graph, search, LLM, OKF-export, and analytical derivatives; never new authority.

Do not redistribute material merely because it was available to an agent. Public templates contain no private corpus. Quotation remains minimal and purpose-bound. Personal or confidential data is excluded, redacted, pseudonymized, or kept in a declared access-controlled profile.

Deletion and correction must propagate from the authoritative tier through dependent canonical concepts and regenerated outputs. Logs retain a safe audit record without preserving deleted sensitive content.
