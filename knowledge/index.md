# DenchCo standard knowledge bundle

This first-class OKF bundle is the portable concept layer for the standard. Normative wording remains in the linked specification; these concepts provide stable identities, provenance, lifecycle, and traversal without requiring the rendered site.

- [Standard](concepts/standard.md) — purpose, scope, and normative resource.
- [Evidence governance](concepts/evidence-governance.md) — authority and uncertainty model.
- [Human and LLM Wikis](concepts/human-and-llm-wikis.md) — coordinated products over shared knowledge.
- [Production profile](concepts/production-profile.md) — Zensical, DenchCo style, Graphify, and executable verification.
- [Update protocol](concepts/update-protocol.md) — release-pinned, reviewable evolution.
- [Bundle log](log.md) — chronological bundle changes.
