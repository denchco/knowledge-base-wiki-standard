# Wiki Standard

Use the target repository's `AGENTS.md`, `.wiki-standard.yaml`, declared specification, LLM entry point, and project status. They define its selected profile and local choices. This plugin supplies workflow guidance, not normative authority or a subject corpus.

For a new Wiki, resolve an immutable revision of `https://github.com/denchco/knowledge-base-wiki-standard` and follow its `prompts/instantiate-wiki.md` URL-only adaptive discovery. Create an independent repository and subject-owned knowledge. Preserve the user's requirements, existing evidence and stronger local rules.

For new original prose, use the target's mapped authoring policy and its pinned Standard. Where that revision adopts the ASD-STE100-inspired 80% default, apply it as a pragmatic style preference. Preserve meaning, exact source forms and local requirements. Do not claim a computed or official compliance score, impose a newer policy on an older pin, or trigger a retrospective rewrite.

For maintenance, use Graphify query-first when available, then the smallest canonical source set needed. Preserve unknown OKF fields and deviations. Register evidence before using it as authority. Use the pinned Standard's read-only conformance CLI and report applicable checks honestly. Never infer release publication or consumer adoption from an accepted proposal.

Finish the requested scope. Ask material clarifications one at a time. Under an adopted revision with the development-cycle policy, assess the continuing Wiki subject, evidence and content maturity after every development cycle. Suggest no more than three prioritised content-first updates using `Proposed update:`, `Why it matters:` and `Done when:`, or state `No further grounded content update is available.` A failed or unrun required check takes priority over optional content. Check relevant handoff evidence and do not repeat completed work. Reopen parked work only with its declared trigger, new evidence or explicit instruction. Suggestions do not authorise execution and must not become filler. Older pinned consumers retain their adopted policy. Use the target's verified canonical HTTP(S) Wiki origin for reader navigation.

The bundled Stop hook is optional. It does nothing without the project's explicit `.codex/wiki-standard-stop.json` opt-in and host hook trust. Packaging or installation does not authorise trust, enablement, marketplace edits or settings changes. Follow the pinned Standard's `docs/llm-wiki/codex-adapter.md` only when setup is requested. Its checks cover captured message links and handoff structure; the agent must still assess goal relevance, completion, provenance and evidence. A hook warning is not a verification pass.
